Poem October 20, 2026

5 Year

RA thousand roads could call my name, a thousand souls could pass me by, yet every heartbeat stops and starts at just the thought of you and I. Not drawn by fleeting, shallow grace, nor empty wants that fade and die, but with the ancient, steady weight of Eden under quiet sky— like Adam looking down to find the very breath he'd lacked before, the piece that made his spirit whole, the half of him he loved the more. And even gods would leave the stars to watch you in the fading light, while silent angels lose their words before the wonder of our night. So I don't want to just love you, I want to learn everything about you— to hear every piece of your story, to know every corner you hide. To make you repeat my name until your voice breaks soft and low, and undress you like a secret page where only my hands get to go. To stare into your eyes and watch your body fold beneath my touch, to kiss your skin from breath to breath, and love you deep, and love you much— to lay you down so carefully, yet make you feel high, recklessly. Through every year a turning page, through every day a sacred verse, through all the winding paths we take, it’s you I choose, and choose, and choose. Forever yours, Hugo
Built with all my love ❤️
